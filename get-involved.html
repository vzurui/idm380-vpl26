GET INVOLVED

There are many ways to lend Harlem Grown your time, energy, and passion.

From internships to volunteering and community service, we have opportunities available for individuals High School-aged and up.

Partner With Us

Invest in Harlem Grown’s mission and make a lasting impact. Engage your employees through immersive, hands-on volunteer experiences at our urban farms while strengthening your brand’s commitment to social change and sustainability. Partner with us to amplify your impact, enhance visibility, and inspire communities through meaningful action.

Click here to partner with us.

 

Volunteer

Each growing season (April-October), we love to welcome volunteers from our community to help with tasks on our farms. No farming skills are necessary! All you need is to bring yourself and a positive, can-do attitude. 

Click here to sign up.

Internships

Develop professional skills and learn about the inner-workings of a community nonprofit organization. Internships are available in agriculture, programming, operations, development, and marketing.

*We are currently not accepting internship applications. Please check back in the fall for updates on future opportunities.*
